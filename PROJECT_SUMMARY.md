# Project Summary

## Objective
Analyze U.S. county-level health-insurance coverage from 2017-2022 and identify descriptive disparities across socioeconomic and demographic categories.

## Data
U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey 5-year S2701 health-insurance coverage extracts for 2017 through 2022.

## Dimensions analyzed
Age, disability status, educational attainment, work experience, income, poverty ratio, and county geography.

## Analytical outputs
Descriptive-statistics tables, 95% confidence intervals, annual trend charts, dual-axis charts, income and poverty comparisons, and insured/uninsured county heat maps.

## Reported conclusion
Coverage generally improved over the study period, but meaningful disparities persisted for working-age adults, lower-income populations, people with lower educational attainment, part-time workers, and other vulnerable groups. The project is descriptive and should not be presented as establishing causal policy effects.
