# Methodology Summary

The submitted report states that SAS was used to compile, clean, analyze, and visualize county-level ACS health-insurance data from 2017 through 2022.

## Documented analytical steps
1. Compile six annual ACS S2701 county-level extracts.
2. Retain demographic and socioeconomic coverage variables for insured and uninsured populations.
3. Address incomplete or missing observations for consistent year-to-year comparisons.
4. Create subgroup summaries for age, disability, education, work experience, income, and poverty categories.
5. Produce descriptive statistics including N, mean, standard deviation, and lower/upper 95% confidence limits for the mean.
6. Create annual insured/uninsured trend figures.
7. Produce county-level geographic heat maps for insured and uninsured populations.
8. Interpret observed disparities in a health-policy context.

## Important interpretation boundary
The report sometimes discusses possible policy explanations for observed trends. The supplied artifacts support descriptive association and trend analysis; they do not document a causal identification strategy. Public portfolio wording should therefore use terms such as `associated with`, `corresponded with`, or `coincided with`, rather than causal claims.
